Solace is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, and to a leasing experience that welcomes all.

Our commitment

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and we treat accessibility as an ongoing part of how this site is built and maintained.

What we’ve done

  • Semantic, screen-reader-friendly page structure
  • Descriptive alternative text for images
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus states
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences
  • Legible type and considered color contrast
  • A responsive layout that works on phones, tablets and desktops

Accessible residences

For information about accessible homes and features at 74 St Marks, please contact our leasing team — we’re glad to help.

Need assistance?

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or would like information in another format, email hello@85fourth.com. Tell us the page and the issue, and we’ll help and work to fix it.
